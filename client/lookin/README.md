LookIn > Look inside the design.


LookIn is an interactive architectural presentation platform designed to help architects and designers present floor plans and their associated spaces in a more informative and engaging way.

Instead of viewing a floor plan as a static image, LookIn allows users to explore individual rooms through interactive room markers and view their corresponding images, areas, windows, and other details.

Features

Interactive floor-plan presentation

Clickable room markers

Individual room information and images

Room area and window details

Architectural design/project descriptions

Structured presentation of spaces within a design

Responsive web interface

3D view — under development


How It Works

Architectural Design
        ↓
    Floor Plan
        ↓
  Select a Room
        ↓
Room Image & Details

A design can contain a floor plan along with information about its individual rooms. Users can select a room directly from the floor plan and explore the information associated with that space.

Current Status

LookIn is currently under development.

The core interactive flow — Floor Plan → Room Selection → Room Image & Details — has been implemented. Additional functionality, including the 3D architectural view, is currently being developed.

Purpose

LookIn was developed as part of the AS-08 hackathon with the goal of creating a more interactive way to present and explore architectural designs.
